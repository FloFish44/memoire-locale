How to find duplicate files in Windows

Identify duplicate documents and photos by size, content and location before deleting anything.

Compare more than filenames

Different names can contain identical data, while matching names can contain different versions.

Check size and content

A cryptographic hash is a reliable way to confirm byte-for-byte duplicates.

Review every location

Keep the copy in the clearest folder and check dates before removing another copy.

Back up before cleanup

Avoid permanent deletion until you know which version is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does Retrio delete duplicates automatically?

No. Automatic duplicate cleanup is planned for a future version.

Are similar photos exact duplicates?

Not necessarily. Similar-looking photos may have different resolution, edits or metadata.

Find files using your own words

Retrio only analyses the folders you choose. Your files remain on your computer.
