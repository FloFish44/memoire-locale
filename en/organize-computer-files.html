How to organize files on your computer

A simple method for organizing Documents, Downloads and photos without creating a complicated folder system.

Use a few clear categories

Start with broad folders such as Personal, Work, Finance and Photos.

Name files consistently

Include a subject and date when useful, for example EDF_invoice_2026-02.pdf.

Process Downloads regularly

Move useful files and delete items you no longer need after checking them.

Keep search available

Good organization and content search work together when a filename still escapes you.

Frequently asked questions

How many folders should I create?

Use the smallest number that makes your files easy to understand.

Should I rename every old file?

No. Start with important and frequently used files.

Find files using your own words

Retrio only analyses the folders you choose. Your files remain on your computer.
