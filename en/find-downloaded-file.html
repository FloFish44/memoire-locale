Where can I find a downloaded file?

Locate a file you downloaded but can no longer find using your browser history, the Downloads folder or content search.

Open Downloads

Press Windows+E and select Downloads in File Explorer.

Check browser downloads

Use your browser download history to see the filename and open its folder.

Sort by date

Show the newest files first when the download was recent.

Search by content

If the filename is unclear, search for words expected inside the document.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my download missing?

It may have been saved to a different folder, renamed automatically or removed by the browser or security software.

Can Retrio search Downloads?

Yes, when you select Downloads before running the analysis.

Find files using your own words

Retrio only analyses the folders you choose. Your files remain on your computer.
